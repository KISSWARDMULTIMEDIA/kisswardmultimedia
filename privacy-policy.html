Privacy Policy

How Kissward Multimedia collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit our website or engage our services.

Last updated: 5 June 2026

01. Introduction

Kissward Multimedia ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at www.kisswardmultimedia.top and when you engage our multimedia, software development, design, or IT support services.

We operate in accordance with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for visitors from the European Economic Area. By using our website and services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

02. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: (1) information you provide directly, (2) information collected automatically through cookies and tracking technologies, and (3) information from third-party services.

Information You Provide

  • Contact details — your name, email address, phone number, and project details when you fill out our contact form or reach out via WhatsApp.
  • Project materials — files, images, videos, brand guidelines, and other assets you share with us for the purpose of delivering our services.
  • Communications — records of your correspondence with us via email, WhatsApp, phone, or our website contact form.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, the following information may be collected automatically:

03. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse site traffic, serve targeted advertisements, and understand where our visitors come from. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser.

Types of cookies we use:

Category Purpose Examples Duration
Essential Required for basic site functionality (session management, load balancing) Session cookies Session
Analytics Understand how visitors interact with our site (pages visited, time on site) Google Analytics (_ga, _gid, _gat) Up to 2 years
Advertising Serve relevant ads, track conversions, limit ad frequency Meta Pixel, Google AdSense cookies Up to 180 days
Session Recordings Record user sessions for UX improvement (anonymised) Microsoft Clarity cookies Up to 12 months

You can control and manage cookies in your browser settings. You may also opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For Google AdSense, you can manage personalised ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

04. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery — to respond to your inquiries, provide quotes, deliver multimedia and software projects, and communicate with you about your projects.
  • Customer support — to assist you with technical issues, service questions, and follow-ups.
  • Analytics & improvement — to analyse website traffic, understand user behaviour, and improve our website content, design, and services.
  • Advertising & remarketing — to show you relevant advertisements about our services on social media and search platforms, and to measure ad performance.
  • Legal compliance — to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes in Ghana and other jurisdictions where we operate.

Legal basis (GDPR): If you are located in the European Economic Area, we process your personal data based on one or more of the following legal bases: (a) your consent, (b) performance of a contract with you, (c) our legitimate interests (analytics, marketing), or (d) compliance with legal obligations.

06. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers — with trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our website, conducting analytics, and delivering services (e.g., Google, Meta, Microsoft). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation in Ghana or applicable jurisdictions.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, we may transfer your data to the acquiring entity.
  • With your consent — we may share your information for any other purpose with your explicit consent.

07. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Specifically:

  • Contact form submissions — retained for up to 24 months after the last communication, unless a longer retention is required for contractual or legal reasons.
  • Analytics data — retained in Google Analytics for 14 months (user-level and event-level data).
  • Project files & materials — retained for the duration of the project and up to 12 months thereafter, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • Communications records — retained for up to 6 years to comply with tax and legal obligations under Ghanaian law.

When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

08. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right Description
Access Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Rectification Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
Erasure Request deletion of your personal data where there is no legitimate reason for us to retain it.
Restriction Request restriction of processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond to your request within 30 days, as required by applicable law.

09. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • Secure HTTPS encryption (SSL/TLS) for all data transmitted between your browser and our website.
  • Restricted access to personal data to only those employees and contractors who need it to perform their duties.
  • Regular review of our data collection, storage, and processing practices.
  • Secure storage of project files and client communications.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately. If we discover that we have collected personal data from a child without verified parental consent, we will delete that information promptly.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. When we make changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your data.

If we make material changes that significantly affect how we handle your personal data, we will notify you via a prominent notice on our website or by direct communication where appropriate.
