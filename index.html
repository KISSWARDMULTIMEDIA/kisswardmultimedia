"Great design is not just what it looks like — it's how it performs, how it connects, and the legacy it leaves behind. That is the Kissward standard." — David Kissward

David Kissward is one of Ghana's most accomplished multimedia professionals and the driving force behind Kissward Multimedia, Accra's premier creative and digital studio. With over a decade of hands-on mastery spanning graphic design, corporate branding, web and app development, 2D & 3D animation, motion graphics, and video production, David has built a reputation for delivering world-class creative work that consistently exceeds client expectations.

A visionary leader with a rare blend of technical brilliance and artistic intuition, David has led the conceptualisation and execution of 500+ projects for clients ranging from government institutions and NGOs to corporate brands, event organizers, and individual entrepreneurs across Ghana and internationally. His expertise has shaped festival identities, broadcast graphics, product packaging, award-winning animations, and high-converting digital platforms — all from his studio in Accra.

As a certified graphic designer and brand strategist, David's approach to corporate branding goes beyond aesthetics — he architects visual identities that communicate authority, trust, and purpose. His website and app development work integrates cutting-edge UI/UX principles with robust technical architecture, ensuring every digital product is fast, SEO-optimised, and built to convert. In animation, his 2D character work and cinematic 3D renders have been featured in national broadcasts and international campaigns alike.

David founded Kissward Multimedia with a singular mission: to democratise access to premium creative services in Africa, and to prove that world-standard multimedia work can be produced right here in Accra, Ghana. Under his leadership, the studio has become the go-to creative partner for brands that refuse to settle for ordinary.
