Over 500 projects for government institutions, NGOs, corporate brands, event organisers and independent founders — festival identities, broadcast graphics, packaging, animation and digital platforms, all produced here.

08 FAQ

Before you
get in touch.

Straight answers about working with a studio in Accra — scope, timelines, pricing and remote collaboration.

What services does Kissward Multimedia offer in Accra?

Professional video editing, custom website development, graphic design and branding, 2D and 3D animation, motion graphics, IT support and PC repair, social media management and content creation, plus mobile and desktop app development — all delivered from our studio in Accra, Ghana.

How much does a website cost in Ghana?

Website development costs in Ghana vary with complexity. Kissward Multimedia builds custom websites at competitive rates. Message us on WhatsApp at +233 54 260 3997 for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your project.

Do you work with clients outside Accra?

Yes. We are based in Accra but work with clients nationwide across Ghana and internationally. Projects are delivered remotely with scheduled reviews, so location is never a barrier.

Do you develop mobile and desktop applications?

We build Android and iOS apps as well as Windows, macOS and Linux desktop software — from customer-facing products to internal business tools. Two of our apps are live on Google Play.

Why choose Kissward Multimedia?

Over a decade of professional practice, 500+ delivered projects and a team that combines Ghanaian creativity with international production standards. You work directly with the founder from brief to delivery.
